## 📖 About The Project

**EventRide** is a modern and user-friendly platform designed for groups of friends planning to attend events together - concerts, football matches, picnics, or any other gathering. Split taxi costs fairly and travel together!

### 🎯 Key Goals
- **Save Money** - Share taxi costs and save up to 50%
- **Convenience** - Multiple people ride in one taxi
- **Transparency** - Clear overview of who paid what and who owes
- **Eco-Friendly** - Fewer cars = lower CO2 emissions
